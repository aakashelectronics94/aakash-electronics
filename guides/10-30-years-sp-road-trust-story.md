# 30 Years on SP Road: Why Aakash Electronics Is Bengaluru's Trusted Component Sourcing Partner

Most electronics suppliers come and go with market cycles. Aakash Electronics has operated from the same counter on SP Road, Bengaluru, since 1994, through the rise of the internet, the growth of India's electronics manufacturing base, and the shift from analog hobby projects to IoT and robotics. This is the story of how a single retail counter became a sourcing partner for manufacturing units, government organisations, and educational institutions across India.

## How It Started: A Retail Counter for Passives and ICs

In 1994, Mr. Kiran Kumar opened a small counter on SP Road selling passive components and ICs. SP Road was already establishing itself as Bengaluru's electronics trading hub, and the shop's early philosophy was simple: stock the right parts, charge a fair price, and treat every question, whether about a single resistor or a bulk order, with the same seriousness.

## 1995: The First B2B Clients

Within a year, word had spread beyond retail customers. Manufacturing units and government organisations in Bengaluru's industrial belt began placing bulk orders, and the shop formally became Aakash Electronics: no longer just a retail counter, but a procurement partner for businesses that needed components reliably and at scale.

## The 2000s: Expanding Into Wires & Cables

As Bengaluru's infrastructure and construction activity grew through the 2000s, Aakash Electronics expanded into a full range of wires and cables to serve contractors, automation integrators, and industrial electricians, a category that remains a core part of the business today.

## The 2010s: A Generation of Engineering Students

The 2010s brought a new customer base: engineering students. Aakash Electronics launched curated kits for first-year electronics coursework, and the radio assembly kit became one of the top-selling educational products in the region, a project that introduced a generation of Bengaluru's engineering students to building their first working circuit.

## Today: One Counter, Every Kind of Customer

Three decades later, Aakash Electronics stocks components across twelve categories, from passives and semiconductors to development boards, sensors, robotics kits, PCBs, tools, and power supplies, serving three distinct groups under one roof:

- **Manufacturing units and MSMEs** sourcing bulk components and BOM fulfillment for production
- **Government organisations** placing institutional orders
- **Educational institutions** equipping labs, robotics clubs, and individual students

## What Hasn't Changed

Mr. Kiran Kumar still runs the shop personally from the same SP Road counter. The philosophy from day one, stock the right parts, charge a fair price, and take every question seriously, has outlasted three decades of change in how electronics get bought, sold, and built. In an industry where sourcing relationships often come down to trust and responsiveness as much as price, that continuity is the actual product being sold.

## Why This Matters for Buyers

For a manufacturing unit choosing a bulk supplier, or a college procurement committee evaluating vendors, longevity is a genuine signal: a supplier who has served industrial, government, and educational clients for 30+ years has weathered supply chain disruptions, component shortages, and shifting technology (from analog ICs to IoT boards) without losing the trust of repeat customers. That's a different kind of credibility than a new entrant can offer, regardless of how polished a website looks.
